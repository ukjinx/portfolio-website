About Me

First off, welcome to my little slice of the internet, where passion meets the lens. Since 2008, I have been on a journey behind the camera, capturing the essence of the world’s landscapes and wildlife.

I am Joe Jenkinson, a dedicated landscape and wildlife photographer based in the UK.

The Journey Behind the Lens

My photographic journey began in 2008, sparked by a deep appreciation for the outdoors.

Armed with a camera, I set out to capture awe-inspiring landscapes and fascinating wildlife from across the UK and beyond.

Over the years, photography has taken me from rolling hills and quiet lakes to remote wilderness locations around the world.

Photography as a Path to Wellbeing

Photography has also become an important part of my mental health journey.

After struggling with depression for many years, I found peace in slowing down and reconnecting with nature through the camera.

Walking through forests, mountains, and coastlines allows me to focus entirely on the present moment.

Photography became my form of mindfulness — a way to breathe, observe, and appreciate the world around me.

The Great Outdoors as My Muse

Nature continues to inspire every photograph I create.

Whether it’s the glow of sunrise across a landscape or the raw presence of wildlife in its natural habitat, I aim to capture emotion, atmosphere, and connection.

Beyond Boundaries

While my roots are firmly planted in the UK, my photography has taken me far beyond it.

I believe beauty exists everywhere — from rugged British coastlines to distant wilderness landscapes across the world.

Every journey becomes an opportunity to tell a different story.

Join Me on This Journey

Photography is more than taking pictures. It’s about sharing experiences, emotions, and moments that might otherwise be forgotten.

Through my work, I hope to encourage others to slow down, reconnect with nature, and see the world differently.

Thank you for visiting Joe Jenkinson Photography.

Capturing Moments,
Joe Jenkinson
